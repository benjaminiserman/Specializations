Specializations adds more options to the hardmode metal equipment to fill neglected niches in weapon progression.

What Specializations offers:
* 16 new guns
* 8 new rapiers
* 8 new throwing weapons

Changelog:

v0.5.1:
+ Updated to Terraria 1.4.4
* Update thumbnail
* Rebalanced all weapons
* Removed throwing helmets, merged throwing weapons into ranged
* Reworked some weapon mechanics

v0.4:
+ Added 8 new throwing weapons
+ Cobalt Rapier gives swiftness on hit
+ Mythril Rapier gives sharpness on hit
* Fixed bug where Rapier Bleed debuff lasts forever on NPCs

v0.3:
* First public release
* Fixed bug where mythril weapons could be made on a normal anvil
+ Added throwing armor helmets
